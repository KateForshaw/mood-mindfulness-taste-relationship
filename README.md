# This was my dissertation project for my undergraduate psychology degree. I conducted a behavioural study where participants tasted foods and completed questionnaires measuring taste perception, mood, and mindfulness.
# Descriptive statistics and tests of normality were performed to assess the data's suitability for parametric and non-parametric methods.
# I ran paired-sample t-tests and Wilcoxon signed-rank test to compare taste ratings across conditions - negative mood and positive mood.
# Pearson's and Spearman's correlations were conducted to explore taste perception's relationship with mood states and mindfulness scores.
# The attached files consist of my research report summarising and analysing the findings, and the appendeces containing SPSS outputs and essential documents for the study.
